How This Dashboard Works

Column definitions, formulas, role presets, and status logic — all in one place

Column Definitions

Loan AmountTotal loan value for the deal
Gross Comm %Commission rate — syncs bidirectionally with Gross $
Gross Comm ($)Total commission before any deductions
Processing CostDeducted from Gross before splitting (lender fees etc.)
Net CommissionGross Comm ($) minus Processing Cost
Split 1Your % share of Net Comm (or $995 flat for Processing roles)
Split 250% split with Jon · 100% solo
JY CommissionYour final take-home on this deal

Commission Formulas

Gross $ = Loan Amount × Gross %
Gross % ↔ Gross $ auto-sync when either changes
Net Commission = Gross $ − Processing Cost
JY Commission = Net Comm × Split 1 × Split 2
Processing Role exception:
JY Commission = $995 × Split 2
Flat fee — Net Comm is ignored for split calc

Role Presets

Juan Self SourcedS1: 75% · S2: 100%
Juan Company SourcedS1: 25% · S2: 100%
Juan LOAS1: 10% · S2: 100%
Juan ProcessingS1: $995 flat · S2: 100%
Juan LOA with JonS1: 10% · S2: 50%
Juan Processing with JonS1: $995 flat · S2: 50%

Selecting a role auto-fills Split 1 & 2. You can always override manually.

Status & Table Movement

Projected Avg comp (1.75%) estimate · stays in Pipeline
Estimated Commission entered manually · stays in Pipeline
Closed – Estimated Deal closed, payment pending · moves to Closed
Closed – Paid Deal closed and paid · moves to Closed

Set a Closed deal back to "Projected" or "Estimated" to return it to Pipeline. All changes auto-save to your browser.

Sourcing Categories

JY Sourced
Role = Juan Self Sourced — deals you brought in directly. Split 1 is 75%.
Helm Sourced
All other roles or blank — Company Sourced, LOA, Processing. Sourced through Helm.

The JY vs Helm split flows through the KPI cards, progress bar, monthly chart, and 2026 Outlook automatically — set the Role column and everything updates.
